How to use UnderwriteIQ

1
Connect your API Key Open API Settings, select your preferred LLM provider (OpenRouter, Claude, or OpenAI), and paste your key.
2
Upload Financial Documents Drag in any bank statement, tax return, or MCA application in PDF, PNG, or JPG format. Multi-page statements are automatically processed.
3
Run AI Extraction & Math Audit Click Extract. The engine extracts all line items and runs an exact mathematical reconciliation against the statement totals.
4
Dual-Pane Inspection & Export Click View Data to review transactions side-by-side with the original document. Filter by MCA flags or export to CSV / JSON.
Security Note: Uploaded files and keys are processed securely in memory and in your browser's IndexedDB. Never retained in remote databases.
